About Meghrod

A homestay shaped by weather, wood, and welcome.

Nestled on a ridge where monsoon clouds drift through the pines, Meghrod is a family-run stay built for slow mornings and long evenings. Rooms open onto gardens. Meals are cooked from the day’s harvest. Nothing here is rushed — except perhaps the kettle.

Whether you arrive for a weekend unplug or a week of trail walking, you are hosted, not checked in. Unwind. Breathe. Belong.

Complimentary for every guest FREE WiFi

Stay connected in rooms, gardens, and the deck — high-speed internet included with your stay.
